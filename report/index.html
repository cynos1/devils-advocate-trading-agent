Interpretation

What worked — and what did not

Strongest behavior
Risk escalation
Flawed proposals were separated from clean controls well enough to calibrate intervention thresholds from observed severity rather than guesses.
Known limitation
Exact labels
The adversary matched the injected failure-mode label only 12/24. It is a calibrated escalation layer, not an infallible classifier.
Authority boundary

What models may and may not do

AI is allowed to reason. It is not allowed to redefine its own permissions.
Models may
  • Propose trades
  • Criticize reasoning
  • Suggest one revision
  • Abstain
Models may not
  • Bypass contract caps
  • Create naked calls
  • Exceed collateral or coverage
  • Override the daily loss halt
  • Remove the kill switch
  • Bypass the fresh execution gate
